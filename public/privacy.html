Privacy notice

Blu Chip Compliance Ltd (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Contact: info@bc-compliance.co.uk, 07775 507073.

What we collect

When you use our quote request or appointment forms we collect the details you enter: names, company, address and contact details for you and other project parties (such as agents, designers and contractors), project and site information, and any files you upload.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation (legitimate interests / steps at your request before entering a contract).
  • To carry out building control services, including preparing and submitting Initial Notices to local authorities (performance of a contract; legal obligations under the Building Act 1984 and the Building Safety Act 2022).
  • To keep records required by building control legislation and our insurers (legal obligation / legitimate interests).

Who we share it with

Local authorities, fire and rescue services, sewerage undertakers and other statutory consultees where required; the Building Safety Regulator; our insurers and professional advisers; and IT and email providers acting on our behalf. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Building control records are retained for as long as is required by law and our professional obligations – this can be many years. Enquiries that do not proceed are kept for a shorter period.

Your rights

You have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). Contact us to exercise them.

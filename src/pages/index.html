Your choice of building control

A private alternative to the local authority

Almost all building work needs building control sign-off. In England you can choose who provides it: your local authority, or a Registered Building Control Approver (RBCA) such as Blu Chip Compliance.

The standards are exactly the same – the Building Regulations apply whichever you choose. What changes is the service: one named point of contact, flexible site visits, practical advice and a consistent approach, whichever council area your project is in.
